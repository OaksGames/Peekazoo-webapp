The device

Meet Piko.

Peek into the magic playworld.

A soft blue bunny with a camera inside. Piko clips onto the TV, sees your child move, and turns the living-room rug into the playground.

Setting up

Two steps, then you're playing.

1

Plug in and pair

One cable in, and Piko finds your screen on its own. Ready in about two minutes.

2

Step back and play

Your child appears on screen and the game begins. No controller. No touch.

100% kid-safe experience

Camera-based play with no online access, no ads and nothing to buy. Nothing your child does in front of Piko leaves the room.

Parent concerns?

We've got answers.

Too much screen time?

It's active, not passive. Kids move, play and engage the whole way through — and they're usually out of breath by the end.

Not enough moving about?

Our games get them jumping, reaching, stretching and exploring — the movements preschool teachers ask for.

Is it educational?

Yes. Every game builds coordination, focus, counting and problem solving — quietly, while they think they're just playing.

Is it safe?

Camera-based play with no online access. Kid-safe and parent-friendly, with nothing to tap and nobody to talk to.

Together time

The best games are better together.

Piko is made for the whole family. Two players can step in front of the camera, so grown-ups can join the fun, help when things get tricky, and dance along too. No controllers, no waiting for your turn—just play together.

Questions parents ask.

Does it work on any TV?+

Piko clips onto the top edge of any television, or sits on a shelf nearby. Smart TV, old TV, the one at the grandparents' — all the same to us.

How much room do we need?+

About two big steps of space in front of the TV. Small living rooms are the normal case, not the exception.

Does it need internet?+

Only to bring home new games. The games already in the box play happily offline.

Can two children play?+

Yes. The camera sees both of them, and the games hand the turn from one to the other.
